What is happening

A deliberately plain 3-D U-Net is the starting point: min-max normalization, uniform 32³ patches, plain cross-entropy, Adam at a flat 1e-3, no augmentation, three levels of 16/32/64 channels. Trained for 600 s per seed on one GPU it scores 0.8753 mean Dice on the frozen validation split (mean of seeds 1337 and 2027, averaged over 3 calibration pairs). Re-running that unchanged recipe moves the two-seed mean by up to 0.0011, so the keep rule demands a gain above 0.0022, twice that scatter. A fresh pair that took no part in setting the threshold scored +0.0002 and was correctly not kept.

Two arms climb from that point under the same budget and the same rule, alternating one experiment each. The agent reads the baseline and its own rows, edits one knob in recipe.py, and says why. The random-edit control draws one mutation from the same knob space. Neither sees the hold-out split. The pre-registered claim is 0.890 within 25 experiments per arm, with at least three kept edits that map to named nnU-Net components; the kill condition is the control matching the agent within the noise floor.

So far: the agent has run 1 experiment and kept 1, moving its incumbent to 0.8800 (+0.0047 over the baseline). The control has run 1 and kept 0, incumbent 0.8753.

Kept by the agent:

  • #30 aug_flip off -> on: 600 s overtrains the flat-LR baseline (0.875 vs 0.878 at 300 s), mirroring regularises and was the best dry-run edit (+0.006 on seed 1337) (nnU-Net: mirroring augmentation): seeds 0.8797, 0.8802, mean 0.8800.

History: at a 300 s single-seed budget (rows 1 to 21) the same baseline scored 0.8777 and the same-seed scatter forced a 0.011 gate under which all ten dry-run edits reverted. The campaign bought resolution with two seeds at 600 s. The 600 s baseline is lower than the 300 s one: the flat schedule does not turn extra steps into Dice, which is itself a recipe fact.
